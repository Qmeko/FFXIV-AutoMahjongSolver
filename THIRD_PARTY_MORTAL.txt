Third-party Mortal runtime
==========================

BUILD_DEBUG_PLUGIN.bat downloads the current release asset named release4p.zip
from:
  https://github.com/shinkuan/Akagi-MjaiBot-Mortal

That adapter wraps Mortal/libriichi and is distributed separately by its
maintainer. The installer also downloads uv from:
  https://github.com/astral-sh/uv

The public release currently includes a small public/local model intended to
make the integration usable and testable. It is not represented here as the
strongest available Mortal model. This source ZIP does not redistribute the
Mortal runtime, PyTorch, uv, or model weights; the BAT retrieves them from their
respective upstream release/package sources on the user's machine.

Review each upstream project's license before redistribution.
