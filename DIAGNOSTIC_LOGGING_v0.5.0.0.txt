Doman Mahjong Solver Debug v0.5.0.0

Added /xllog decision diagnostics:
- [MahjongSnapshot]
- [OpponentSnapshot]
- [MortalInput]
- [MortalDecision]

The entries are emitted only when the policy is asked for a decision, not every frame.
Settings > Developer > Diagnostic decision logging (/xllog) controls the feature.
Default: ON for this diagnostic build.
